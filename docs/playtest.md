# Playtest

Test scenarios for playing the game by hand. They give away these deals, so use other seeds if
you want to play blind. Start the game from the root of the repository with `npm run play`, as in
[Play](../README.md#play).

## What comes back

This takes about fifteen minutes. Each row starts at the address shown. Adding `&month=N` to an address only works
in the browser tests' own build, so play from month 1 and do the same as the row.

| Seed and what to do                                                                                                                           | Expect                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## Rivals

This takes about ten minutes. Nobody is ever called a Rival until the results screen.

| Seed and what to do                                                            | Expect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seed 39. File the report and go to the meeting each month                      | As month 1 closes, someone in the kitchen asks how you are coping: your project is said to be in trouble. Nothing says who started it.                                                                                                                                  |
| The same run, month 8. At the Listening Session                                | You can now tell the Office Co-ordinator that the Project Co-ordinator's project is in trouble. You have seen it done, and yours is the same Move: a false story that travels from whoever you tell, until someone sees through it and works out that it was you.       |
| The same run, to the end                                                       | The results screen lists who was competing with you, for what, from when, and each Move they made against you. Under what each of them believes, it shows who made the story up, who heard it from whom, and anyone who later worked out that it was not true.          |
| Seed 22, month 8. Play plainly to the Listening Session                        | Nobody spread a story about you, so you have no such option.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Any seed. Play plainly and watch the Moves that land on you                    | Some months, a colleague takes your credit, puts a delay on you in the minutes or asks a pointed question upstairs, and is named. Your boss can be the one. A Practitioner is likeliest, but anyone competing can do it, so the results screen credits only the Rivals. |
| Seed 5. In month 4, go to the tool demonstration, then play plainly to month 6 | You learn "single source of truth", and the New Metric review lets you ask the Senior Analyst how their number was arrived at. Without it, or seeing such a question asked, you cannot.                                                                                 |

## Grand Plans

This takes about ten minutes. Each row starts at the address shown, and goes to the meeting in
month 1, where your boss's Grand Plan is announced. From then on it is on the **Your project** card.

| Seed and what to do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Expect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Seed 106, a Believer boss. In month 4, tell your boss the tool is rolled out                                                                                                                                                                                    | The Plan is completed at once, in appearance, and your boss takes it badly. Official standing still goes up. A month or two later the Head of Operations may go looking for the figures in the tool. In month 5 your boss has a new Grand Plan, Hot-desking. |
| Seed 283, an Incompetent boss. The same                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Your boss is delighted, and forwards the launch e-mail upstairs.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Seed 37, a Practitioner boss. Watch month 2's Lessons Learned Review for who has the real figures. In month 4 go to the demonstration, in month 5 ask that person for the figures, and at the Ideas Workshop offer to load them so that your boss can launch it | It is completed for real, and your boss is pleased. Launching it yourself instead goes down less well with this boss. Asking anyone else gets you last year's figures: it looks done, and is not. In month 4, hand the tool to the Senior Analyst, then play plainly                                                                                                                                                                                    | In month 7 the Plan has failed, and a write-up says who was to blame. On some seeds it is you, and that is a big mistake. Asking your boss which problems it solves keeps you out of it.                                                                     |

## Three kinds of boss

This takes about twenty minutes. Each seed deals a different kind of boss:

| Seed                              | Your boss      | Try                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `http://localhost:5173/?seed=106` | A Believer     | Do the work, tell the truth, and stay away from the theatre    |
| `http://localhost:5173/?seed=283` | An Incompetent | Slides, confidence and buzzwords, and the green report         |
| `http://localhost:5173/?seed=37`  | A Practitioner | Make your boss look good, offer favours, and do not shine much |

reads and lands differently, and the results screen shows how each of your choices
