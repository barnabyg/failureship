# Playtest

Test scenarios to play by hand, which give away their deals. Start the game with `npm run play`
from the root of the repository, as in [Play](../README.md#play), and add `?seed=N` to the address
it prints. Each row starts in month 1 and plays plainly unless it says otherwise: answer each
e-mail with the report or what costs nothing, go to each month's meeting and say the first thing,
and press Continue. A month takes three or four minutes if you read everything.

## What the balance pass found

Scripted players who know every option played the same 100 deals, seeds 1 to 100, with Company
Events, in `tests/balance/ways-of-playing.test.ts`. A person will likely win less. Of 100 runs:

| Way of playing                                                   | Won | Passed over | Demoted | Fired |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --- | ----------- | ------- | ----- |
| Plainly, as above                                                | 0   | 32          | 0       | 0     |
| Fakes Floor 1's Plan, then does each Plan for real               | 30  | 72          | 0       | 0     |
| Does every Plan for real                                         | 12  | 59          | 0       | 0     |
| Fakes every Plan                                                 | 11  | 64          | 0       | 0     |
| Fakes Floor 1's Plan, then gets rid of each boss                 | 3   | 59          | 9       | 2     |
| Gets rid of every boss                                           | 2   | 39          | 20      | 1     |
| Fakes, gets rid of bosses, and takes the amoral choice each time | 1   | 40          | 4       | 89    |
| Clicks about                                                     | 1   | 52          | 10      | 65    |

Plain play never wins on these deals: it does no Plan, so only a Company Event or a boss leaving
opens a seat for it. Reading people still pays, in `tests/balance/reading-people.test.ts`:

| Whom a climber asks about each open seat | Nobody | A guess | Whoever the signs show holding the room | Whoever really decides |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | ------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Seats won, of those they stood for       | 56.3%  | 66.5%   | 71.8%                                   | 73.0%                  |
| Runs won, of 100                         | 24     | 36      | 44                                      | 48                     |

A run is 18 months but about an hour, not the design's 25 minutes: a script over 200 deals counted
about 245 steps a run, 104 of them a real choice, and 18,800 words before the results screen, which
at 250 to 400 words a minute and 2 seconds a click is 55 to 85 minutes. `monthsPerRun` stays 18, as
the top of Floor 3 is won in months 15 to 18: a shorter run needs less to read. Before steering,
Floor 1's security and metric risks now come out after month 7's shared drive can cover them, and
Floor 3's welcome e-mail offers to ask what the Grand Plan will stop.
